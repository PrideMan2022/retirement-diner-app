Retirement Diner Uploader

Retirement Diner Uploader is a private publishing tool for the YouTube channel The Retirement Diner. The channel owner uses it to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and publishing schedule, and add them to the channel's playlists.

The tool works only with the channel owner's own YouTube account. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.
